Just a quick preface for any readers — this is a website meant to document some of the interesting problems I find myself solving as a developer, or even just typing out some plain topics that I find interesting. And I'm purposefully writing out these articles without AI going through and doing it for me, because I think a lot of the benefit is lost when that's done. I really want to make sure I retain a lot of my opinion and personal judgement as I'm going through typing up any future would-be articles. Just a small tidbit for anyone who happens to be reading.

Thank you!
